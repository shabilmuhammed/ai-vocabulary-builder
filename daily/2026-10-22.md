# Today's Words — 2026-10-22

1. **Dearth** (DURTH) — A lack or shortage of something needed.
   *Example:* There was a dearth of fresh vegetables in the small village during winter.
   *Blank:* After the drought, farmers faced a severe ___ of clean drinking water. | Ethereal, Gaudy, Fortuitous
