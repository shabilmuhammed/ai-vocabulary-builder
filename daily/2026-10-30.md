# Today's Words — 2026-10-30

1. **Abstruse** (ab-STROOS) — Difficult to understand; obscure and complex.

   *Blank:* The professor's theory was so ___ that even other scientists struggled to explain it simply. | Approbation, Benediction, Circumlocution
