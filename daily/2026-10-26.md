# Today's Words — 2026-10-26

1. **Indigenous** (in-DIJ-uh-nus) — Native to a particular place; naturally belonging there, not brought in from somewhere else.
   *Example:* Kangaroos are indigenous to Australia and are not found naturally anywhere else.
   *Blank:* The guide explained that these ___ birds had lived on the island long before any humans arrived. | Prosaic, Odious, Pompous
   *Synonym:* local | foreign, imported, artificial
   *Opposite:* foreign | native, local, domestic
   *Clue:* Belonging naturally to a particular place rather than being brought in from elsewhere. | Prosaic, Odious, Pompous

2. **Hyperbole** (hy-PER-buh-lee) — Exaggerated language used for effect, not meant to be taken literally.
   *Example:* When he said the test was "the hardest thing in the history of the world," that was pure hyperbole.
   *Blank:* Calling the small puddle "an ocean" was obvious ___, meant only to make everyone laugh. | Nadir, Propensity, Platitude
   *Synonym:* overstatement | understatement, fact, silence
   *Opposite:* understatement | exaggeration, overstatement, hype
   *Clue:* Language that stretches the truth on purpose to make a point more dramatic. | Nadir, Propensity, Platitude

3. **Inept** (in-EPT) — Lacking skill; not able to do something competently.
   *Example:* His inept attempt at wiring the lamp left the whole room without power.
   *Blank:* The new waiter was so ___ that he kept mixing up every single order. | Ingenious, Profuse, Pertinent
   *Synonym:* unskilled | skillful, capable, talented
   *Opposite:* skillful | incompetent, clumsy, unable
   *Clue:* Not able to perform a task well because of a lack of skill. | Ingenious, Profuse, Pertinent

4. **Ingenious** (in-JEEN-yus) — Clever, original, and inventive in solving a problem.
   *Example:* She came up with an ingenious way to carry all her books using an old belt.
   *Blank:* Engineers praised the student's truly ___ design for turning waste plastic into bricks. | Inept, Obtuse, Odious
   *Synonym:* resourceful | clumsy, dull, ordinary
   *Opposite:* unoriginal | clever, inventive, brilliant
   *Clue:* Showing a clever, original idea for solving a tricky problem. | Inept, Obtuse, Odious

5. **Juxtapose** (JUK-stuh-pohz) — To place two things side by side so their differences or similarities stand out.
   *Example:* The photographer juxtaposed a crumbling old building with a shiny new skyscraper in one shot.
   *Blank:* The exhibit chose to ___ photos from a hundred years ago next to photos of the same street today. | Prevaricate, Proliferate, Pertinent
   *Synonym:* contrast | separate, hide, ignore
   *Clue:* To put two very different things next to each other so people notice the difference. | Prevaricate, Proliferate, Pertinent

6. **Nadir** (NAY-dur) — The lowest point of something, often used about someone's fortunes or mood.
   *Example:* Losing the championship game in the final second was the nadir of the team's whole season.
   *Blank:* Running out of money during the trip was the absolute ___ of their vacation. | Paragon, Propensity, Prosaic
   *Synonym:* rock bottom | peak, highlight, summit
   *Opposite:* zenith | lowest point, bottom, depth
   *Clue:* The worst or lowest point of a situation. | Paragon, Propensity, Prosaic

7. **Noxious** (NOK-shus) — Harmful or poisonous, especially to breathe.
   *Example:* The factory released noxious fumes that forced everyone nearby to cover their mouths.
   *Blank:* The chemistry teacher warned them never to inhale the ___ gas produced by the experiment. | Palpable, Pompous, Prosaic
   *Synonym:* toxic | harmless, healthy, fresh
   *Opposite:* harmless | poisonous, toxic, dangerous
   *Clue:* Something that is dangerous or poisonous to living things. | Palpable, Pompous, Prosaic

8. **Obtuse** (ob-TOOSS) — Slow to understand something that should be fairly easy to grasp.
   *Example:* He was being deliberately obtuse, pretending not to understand the simple instructions.
   *Blank:* No matter how clearly she explained the rule, he remained completely ___ about it. | Ingenious, Palpable, Pertinent
   *Synonym:* dim-witted | sharp, clever, perceptive
   *Opposite:* perceptive | dull, slow, dense
   *Clue:* Unable or unwilling to understand something that is actually quite simple. | Ingenious, Palpable, Pertinent

9. **Odious** (OH-dee-us) — Extremely unpleasant or hateful.
