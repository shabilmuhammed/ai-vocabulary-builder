# Today's Words — 2026-11-28

1. **Esoteric** (ess-oh-TER-ik) — Known or understood by only a small, specialized group of people.
   *Example:* The professor's lecture on ancient manuscripts was too esoteric for most first-year students.
   *Blank:* The club's rules were so ___ that only the founding members truly understood them. | Gravitas, Eschew, Usurp
   *Synonym:* obscure | Popular, Simple, Widespread
   *Opposite:* mainstream | Obscure, Hidden, Specialized
   *Clue:* Describing knowledge that only a small, specialized group can understand. | Fatuous, Luminary, Rescind

2. **Eschew** (es-CHOO) — To deliberately avoid or keep away from something.

   *Blank:* To stay focused on her studies, she chose to ___ social media during exam week. | Imperious, Gravitas, Luminary

   *Opposite:* embrace | Avoid, Shun, Reject
   *Clue:* To purposely stay away from doing or using something. | Gravitas, Heinous, Maladroit
