# Today's Words — 2026-11-06

1. **Tangential** (tan-JEN-shul) — Only slightly connected to the main topic; a minor side point.
   *Example:* During the meeting, he kept making tangential remarks that had nothing to do with the budget.
   *Blank:* The professor's lecture kept drifting into ___ stories that had little to do with the exam topic. | Forthright, Punctilious, Austerity
   *Synonym:* peripheral | essential, central, obvious
   *Opposite:* central | unrelated, minor, incidental
   *Clue:* Something that relates to the subject only in a minor, indirect way, not the main point. | Forthright, Punctilious, Austerity

2. **Forthright** (FORTH-ryt) — Direct and honest in speech; saying exactly what you think without hiding it.
   *Example:* She gave a forthright answer when asked why the project had failed.
   *Blank:* Rather than making excuses, the coach was ___ about why the team lost. | Tangential, Mawkish, Sycophantic
   *Synonym:* candid | evasive, secretive, dishonest
   *Opposite:* evasive | honest, blunt, direct
   *Clue:* Speaking in a plain, honest way without avoiding the truth. | Tangential, Mawkish, Sycophantic

   *Synonym:* bombastic | simple, plain, modest
   *Opposite:* plain-spoken | wordy, pompous, flowery
   *Clue:* Using big, flowery words meant to sound impressive rather than to communicate clearly. | Forthright, Lackadaisical, Austerity
